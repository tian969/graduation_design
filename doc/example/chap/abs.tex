\begin{cabstract}
随着大型语言模型（LLM）与视觉语言模型（VLM）的快速发展，其在开放域对话与多模态交互场景中的应用不断扩展。然而，现有模型在安全对齐方面仍面临关键挑战：
一方面，安全边界通常依赖隐式学习，难以在安全性与有用性之间取得稳定平衡；
另一方面，训练数据分布不均衡及检索增强机制的不完善，进一步削弱了模型在复杂场景下的安全性与可靠性。
针对上述问题，本文提出一种面向多模态理解的安全增强训练范式，
核心在于将安全规则显式引入模型训练与推理过程，实现安全约束的结构化建模与行为内化。

首先，针对安全边界缺乏显式约束的问题，本文构建了融合规则表示与安全推理链的训练框架，使模型能够学习“规则理解—风险分析—安全生成”的结构化决策过程。
其次，针对多模态数据中图片样本类别不均衡问题，提出基于样本变异的定向数据合成方法，
通过语义保持的多样化变换与拒绝采样机制，扩展长尾风险类别分布，提升模型对复杂视觉风险的泛化能力。
进一步地，针对传统检索增强生成（RAG）在引入外部信息后可能降低安全性的现象，借鉴 Search-R1 思路，
设计基于规则驱动的强化学习机制，引导模型自主决策工具调用与检索行为，在检索过程中融入安全判断，从而提升事实验证场景下的输出安全性与稳定性。
实验结果表明，所提出方法在保持模型有用性的同时显著提升安全性能：
越狱攻击成功率由 72\% 降至 12\%，多模态安全评估得分由 0.56 提升至 0.92。
在检索增强场景中，引入基于规则的工具调用机制后，模型的事实准确率与风险响应合规率均得到明显提升。
研究表明，通过显式建模安全规则并结合数据与决策层面的协同优化，可以有效提升多模态大模型在真实部署环境中的安全对齐能力。

\end{cabstract}

\ifblind\begin{beabstract}\else\begin{eabstract}\fi
With the rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs), their deployment in open-domain dialogue and multimodal interaction scenarios has become increasingly widespread. However, existing models still face critical challenges in safety alignment: (1) safety boundaries are often implicitly learned, making it difficult to balance safety and usefulness; (2) training data suffers from severe distribution imbalance, especially for multimodal risk categories; and (3) retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) can inadvertently degrade output safety by introducing unverified external information.
To address these issues, this paper proposes a multimodal safety enhancement training paradigm that explicitly incorporates safety rules into both training and inference. First, to tackle the lack of explicit safety boundary modeling, we introduce a rule-guided reasoning framework that integrates structured safety rules with safety-aware chain-of-thought supervision, enabling the model to learn a “rule understanding → risk analysis → safe response” decision process. Second, to mitigate the imbalance of image-based risk categories, we propose a sample mutation-based data synthesis approach, which expands long-tail categories through semantic-preserving transformations and rejection sampling, thereby improving the model’s generalization to diverse visual risk scenarios. Furthermore, motivated by the limitations of conventional RAG, we adopt an agentic retrieval paradigm inspired by Search-R1 and design a rule-based reinforcement learning mechanism that enables the model to autonomously decide when and how to invoke retrieval tools, while incorporating safety verification into the retrieval and generation process.
Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach significantly improves safety performance while maintaining strong usefulness. The jailbreak attack success rate is reduced from 0.72 to 0.12, and the multimodal safety score increases from 0.56 to 0.92. In retrieval-augmented scenarios, the proposed agentic and rule-guided mechanism further improves both factual accuracy and safety compliance. These results suggest that explicitly modeling safety rules and jointly optimizing data, reasoning, and decision processes provide an effective solution for robust safety alignment in multimodal large models.
