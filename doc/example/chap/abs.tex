\begin{cabstract}
随着大型语言模型（LLM）与视觉语言模型（VLM）在自然语言理解与跨模态推理能力方面的快速发展，这些模型逐渐被应用于自动化审核、智能客服与辅助决策等实际业务系统。然而，在开放域对话和多模态交互场景中，模型仍面临越狱攻击、违规内容生成以及事实性幻觉等安全风险。如何在保持模型通用能力的同时提升其安全性与稳健性，成为当前的重要研究问题。

本文面向真实部署场景，提出了一种基于规则约束与安全反思的多模态安全增强训练范式。该方法通过构建多模态安全数据合成流水线，生成包含显式规则约束与结构化推理过程的训练数据，使模型学习“规则理解—风险分析—安全生成”的推理模式。在此基础上，设计基于规则验证的在线强化学习机制，通过规则一致性验证与奖励模型相结合，对模型生成结果进行分解式奖励评估，从而将安全约束转化为模型的行为策略。此外，本文进一步探索了在 Agentic RAG 场景下的安全验证机制，以提升模型在检索增强生成过程中的事实可靠性与安全稳定性。

实验结果表明，所提出的方法在保持模型通用能力的同时显著提升了安全性能。多模态越狱攻击的攻击成功率由 0.72 降至 0.12，多模态安全评估得分由 0.56 提升至 0.92，同时保持较高的有用性水平。在 Agentic RAG 场景中，模型的事实准确率与风险响应合规率均得到明显提升。研究结果表明，通过将安全规则显式嵌入模型训练过程，可以有效实现多模态大模型的安全对齐。
\end{cabstract}

\ifblind\begin{beabstract}\else\begin{eabstract}\fi
With the rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) in natural language understanding and cross-modal reasoning, these models are increasingly deployed in real-world systems such as automated content moderation, intelligent assistants, and decision support. However, their deployment also introduces significant safety risks, including jailbreak attacks, generation of policy-violating content, and factual hallucinations in multimodal contexts. Ensuring robust safety boundaries while preserving model utility has therefore become a critical challenge.

This thesis proposes a multimodal safety enhancement training paradigm based on rule constraints and safety reflection. The core idea is to explicitly incorporate safety specifications into the model's reasoning and optimization processes so that safety behaviors can be internalized during generation. A multimodal safety data synthesis pipeline is first constructed to generate training samples containing explicit safety rules, structured reasoning steps, and safe responses. Building upon this dataset, a rule-verification-based online reinforcement learning framework is introduced, which evaluates model outputs using both rule-consistency checks and reward models to guide safe behavioral optimization.

Furthermore, this work investigates safety verification mechanisms in Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) scenarios, analyzing the model's stability when combining retrieval evidence with safety judgments. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach significantly improves safety robustness while maintaining model usefulness. The attack success rate of multimodal jailbreak attacks decreases from 0.72 to 0.12, and the safety score improves from 0.56 to 0.92 while maintaining a helpfulness score of 0.89. In Agentic RAG scenarios, factual accuracy increases from 80.3% to 86.7% with improved compliance in risk-sensitive responses.

These results show that explicitly integrating rule-based reasoning and safety reflection into the training process provides an effective solution for safety alignment in multimodal large models.
